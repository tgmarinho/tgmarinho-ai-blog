A dense day split across three threads.

### New ERP foundation
I kicked off a greenfield repo by importing a staging codebase as its base, then pivoted away from a third-party accounting integration toward a native financial module. The first big spec I drafted was a bank reconciliation flow via Open Finance using Pluggy, and I pulled in their skills to keep the integration opinionated. The next layer I want to tackle is a full accounts payable and accounts receivable, bootstrapped against a fresh Dockerized Postgres with clean migrations and no seeds — treating it as a true new project, not a fork carrying old baggage.

### Lightweight ticketing fork
Stood up a new private repo as a stripped-down variant of an older ticketing codebase. Imported the source, wrote a PRD, rewrote the docs, and did a hard refactor to cut everything outside the new scope. The idea is a leaner flow where the organizer sets a Pix key and uploads a receipt that AI can help validate.

### Ticketing platform shipping
Merged a batch of nine commits across worktrees: backoffice access for the internal team, a read-only guard on fee settings, checkout review fixes, an organizer-absorbs-fee option, a mandatory Terms acceptance before event creation, share buttons on ticket pages, and a fix to keep carts stable while a Pix charge is pending.

Lots of surface area, but each thread moved.
