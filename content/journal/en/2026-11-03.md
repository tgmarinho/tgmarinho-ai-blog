Today was a scouting day more than a shipping day. I spent most of it sketching the shape of a new demo and poking at a couple of exploratory workspaces.

### sunfish

The main thread was figuring out what kind of DEMO would be worth building against a Krea-style brief. I pulled in references (job descriptions, screenshots from the AI Engineer org page) to ground the direction before touching any code. It ended with the initial commit of the repo - just the scaffold, but enough to turn the scattered inspiration into something I can iterate on tomorrow. The goal is a self-contained artifact that shows taste as much as technical range: generative tooling, tight UI, a believable creative loop.

### albany-v1 and nuuk-v1

Both of these are personal-OS experiments living inside my `personal-os` workspace. No commits today - just opening them up, reading references, and thinking about how they could fit together. `nuuk-v1` in particular got me looking at trueup.io as a reference surface for how dense information can feel navigable instead of overwhelming.

Net of the day: one new repo bootstrapped, two others sitting warm in the background. A quiet build-in-public day, but one where the next few days have clearer targets than they did this morning.
