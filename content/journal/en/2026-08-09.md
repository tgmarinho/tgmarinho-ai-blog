A day of steady cleanup on the aviation side and one honest architectural conversation.

### Form work and report resilience

Moved the aircraft form over to React Hook Form with Zod validation. It's the kind of refactor that doesn't look like much from the outside, but it collapses a lot of ad-hoc state handling into one predictable pipeline.

On the reporting side, I fixed two annoyances that had been piling up: legacy decimal values weren't normalizing correctly on the report, and manual saves weren't addressing the draft properly. Also made sure filters survive a back navigation, so I stop losing context every time I dip into a record.

### Municipalities and the shape of an API

Spent time thinking about how city/state selection should flow. The plan: when the owner is picked, the farm follows, and the municipality comes with it, dragging the state along for the report. Small UX detail, big impact on data quality.

### tRPC, decoupling, and honest trade-offs

Had a good back-and-forth about whether tRPC or ORPC would have saved me from the current shape of the codebase. The answer is "probably yes, in some places." But I chose to keep the domain decoupled between frontend and backend, even at the cost of some duplication. Duplication I can see. Coupling hides.

Onward.
