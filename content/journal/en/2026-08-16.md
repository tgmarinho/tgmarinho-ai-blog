Sundays like this one don't leave much of a paper trail in git, but the work still happened.

### cancun

Two sessions in the `cancun` workspace, zero commits. The pattern was familiar: screenshots in, adjustments out. Most of the prompts were images pasted into the loop, with coordinate mappings so the agent could reason about the layout at the original resolution. The only textual instruction of the day was a terse "faz os 2" - do both - which pretty much captures the vibe of a Sunday session: quick decisions, tight feedback loop, no ceremony.

No commits landed, which usually means one of two things: the changes were exploratory and not worth persisting yet, or they're stacked locally waiting for a proper pass before I open a PR. Either way, the value of these sessions is less in the diff and more in the visual iteration - lining things up against the reference, catching small alignment issues, deciding what's worth keeping.

Days like this remind me that "build in public" doesn't only mean shipping. Sometimes it means staring at a screenshot, nudging a spacing token, and moving on. The commits will come tomorrow.

Back to it Monday with something more concrete to show.
