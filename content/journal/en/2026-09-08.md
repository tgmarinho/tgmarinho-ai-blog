Today was less about shipping and more about designing. Zero commits, four repos touched, all conceptual work that will pay off later.

### Receivables domain design

Started sketching a new spec for an accounts receivable module. The goal: let users track receivables inside our system while integrating with an external ERP, but keep the domain layer independent so we can swap providers or roll our own later.

The core model I landed on has three tables: one for the receivable itself (owner, number, due date, status), one for the line items that link back to the operational activity with a partial unique constraint to prevent double-billing, and one for the actual payment events. I also went back and questioned my own naming, "why call it *titulo*?", which is the kind of small friction worth resolving early before the vocabulary calcifies across the codebase.

### Calendar filtering logic

Poked at a scheduling bug where activities from prior months were leaking into the current month view. Walked through it out loud: if today is October 1st and there's nothing scheduled, the view should be empty, not backfilled with September. Sometimes the fix is just stating the invariant clearly.

### Municipal tax integrations

Started mapping which city portals we'd need to integrate with, and whether any national portal covers this. Research mode.
