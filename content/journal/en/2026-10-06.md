Two parallel tracks today, both landing meaningful shifts.

### Billing v2

The big one was `feat(financeiro): faturamento v2` (#844): default category, account per installment, municipality grouping, ISSQN withholding, and contractual retention. On the other repo, `feat(infra): migrate to PostgreSQL, Cloudflare Workers and Neon` (#531) finally landed.
