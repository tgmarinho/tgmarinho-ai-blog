Today was a deep modeling day. Most of the work orbited around one idea: letting multiple companies share the same farm, airstrips, aircraft and general registrations, while staying separate on the accounting side.

### Company groups

Landed the first real slice: shared-per-group registrations, wrapped up and pinned with migration `0020`. Before that, there was a WIP commit that intentionally did not compile — a checkpoint while I reshaped the schema. I also wrote up the specs and diagrams for company groups, the financial layer, and the Conta Azul integration, so the direction is documented and not just in my head.

### Foundations

Pushed two knowledge bases into the repo: a financial-accounting glossary with concepts and insights pulled from Conta Azul, and a full API reference for Conta Azul itself (guides, OpenAPI, summaries). Also dropped SQL POCs exploring company groups and treating the application as the financial atom of the system.

The mental shift of the day: SQL migrations are no longer a real cost to me — agents handle them quickly — so I can optimize purely for the cleanest long-term model instead of trying to avoid schema churn.

### Side repos

A staging PR merged on the iTOP side, and some career doc consolidation on personal-os. Small, but nice to close.
