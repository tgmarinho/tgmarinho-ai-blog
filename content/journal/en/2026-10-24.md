Today was a mix of shipping a small but visible fix and getting the ground ready for a bigger move.

### little-rock-v1

The headline change landed as commit `603c264b`: `fix(analytics): render Google Analytics without @next/third-parties`. Dropping that dependency and rendering GA directly gave me a cleaner integration path and one less abstraction to reason about when something misbehaves in production. It's the kind of fix that looks tiny in the diff but removes a whole class of future surprises.

The rest of the day was pre-flight checks for the production cutover, which is happening today. I verified that the Neon database already holds an up-to-date dump migrated from the previous MongoDB production, which is one less thing to worry about during the switch. I also started mapping how to automate the migration from UploadThing to R2, since I want that fully scripted and reproducible from my local machine rather than clicked together in a dashboard.

Along the way I ran into a recurring friction point: not being able to inspect the actual value of a configured environment variable from the platform UI. It's a small thing until you're staging a cutover and every unknown becomes a risk.

Shipping the fix, aligning the data, and sharpening the migration plan - a good setup day before flipping the switch.
