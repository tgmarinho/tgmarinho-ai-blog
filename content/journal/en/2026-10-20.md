Today was a mix of shipping small but meaningful fixes and doing the groundwork research for bigger integration decisions.

### Reports and backoffice

The headline fix: the DRE report was fusing distinct chart-of-accounts groups when a category moved between groups across companies. That is the kind of silent accounting bug that erodes trust in a report, so getting it isolated and fixed felt good. I also removed a debounce from the backoffice identity search test path - debounces in tests are a smell, and this one was making the suite flaky. On the product side, I renumbered the per-aircraft cost and result docs to align with RF-30.

### Integration research

A lot of the day was spent thinking about third-party ERP sync behavior: how a sync layer detects a changed row, whether the current infrastructure would hold up at ~20k events on accounts payable and receivable, and why the first sync is always slow while subsequent ones get faster once the initial load is done. I also compared trade-offs between leaning on an existing provider for accounts payable/receivable, reconciliation, invoicing, categories and cost centers, versus building more of that in-house if the limitations stack up.

### Infra

Smaller housekeeping: ran a fresh migration end to end on a clean database and bootstrapped the first admin user. Nothing dramatic, but the clean slate felt right.
