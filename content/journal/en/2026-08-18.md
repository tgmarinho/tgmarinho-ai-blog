Today was heavy on shipping across the farm-ops project and lighter on exploration elsewhere.

### Farm-ops platform

The big theme was **offline-first**. I landed structured offline technical reports and pilot reports, made sure authorship is preserved across the offline upgrade path, isolated offline execution per identity, and reinforced sync order so nothing gets scrambled when connectivity comes back. Small but important guardrails when the app has to survive real field conditions.

On the activities side, I optimized the listing for volume with **infinite scroll**, sharpened filters and navigation, and shipped edit, cancel, and duplicate flows (plus a redirect fix on save). A 500 on the dashboard endpoint got squashed with a proper error state and tests around it.

Toolchain got some love too: a **pre-push hook** running `pnpm build` to catch broken builds before they leave the machine, a PR template so reviews start with the right context, and a small docs pass on the members lifecycle inventory. I also did a code review pass on a sibling workspace and turned a couple of findings into follow-up issues.

### Side explorations

Poked at font choices for a UI (landed on Inter), and spent some time thinking about agent-shaped products: what ideas are worth borrowing, why certain tools sell despite commoditized underlying models, and what a leaner alternative for very small teams could look like.
