Today was less about shipping code and more about giving future-me a clean runway. Zero commits, but the planning layer moved forward, which is usually where I lose the most time when I skip it.

### Planning work

I spent the session translating a couple of screenshots and a vague idea into something I can actually execute against. The flow was the usual one I try to stick to:

1. Draft a short PRD/spec from the raw context.
2. Turn that spec into a concrete list of tasks.
3. Land those tasks as GitHub issues (and mirror them under `docs/tickets/` so the repo carries its own memory).

The nice thing about doing this with an agent in the loop is that the "boring" step - fanning the spec out into individual, well-scoped tickets - stops being the bottleneck. I describe the shape of the work once, and I get back a task list I can prune instead of one I have to write from scratch.

### Reflection

Days like this always feel slower than they are. No green squares, no diffs to admire. But turning "I want to build X" into "here are the 8 issues that build X" is the step that decides whether tomorrow is focused work or another round of thinking about thinking. Worth the quiet day.
