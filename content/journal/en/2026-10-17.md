Today was split between adding something new for end users and removing something old that was dragging the codebase down. Two complementary moves: one that makes the product easier to learn, another that makes the code easier to live in.

### Contextual tutorial videos

I shipped a "Teach me" button on the company registration, backoffice records, and activity planning screens. The idea is simple: each screen gets a small helper that opens a short tutorial video explaining how that specific feature works. Hosting the videos as unlisted on YouTube keeps the pipeline cheap, no storage or streaming infra on our side, and the component just embeds them inline when the user asks for help.

It is a lightweight onboarding layer that scales well. Instead of writing documentation nobody reads, I can record a 60-second walkthrough of the real screen and the user watches it in context, right where the question actually appears.

### Dead code cleanup

Alongside the new feature, I deleted a chunk of dead code: leftovers from the previous flow and old HTML prototypes that had been sitting in the repo. Removing code I know is unused is one of the highest-leverage refactors. Less surface area, less noise in search, less cognitive load for the next person reading the file.

Small day, but both commits pulled in the same direction: clarity.
