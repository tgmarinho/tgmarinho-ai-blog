Today was a wide day across several repos, with the ticketing platform getting most of the attention.

### Ticketing platform
A big push landed: an interactive help center at `/docs`, a blog with twelve marketing articles plus an intro piece on why attending events matters, and SEO keywords combined with GEO (FAQ + JSON-LD, sitemap, robots). On the docs side, I clarified that buying a ticket doesn't require an account or login, and detailed the free ASAAS digital account in the organizer onboarding so the KYC step feels less like a wall. On infra, I added a Terraform AWS deploy (ECS Fargate + RDS PostgreSQL) and patched the health check to use Mongo `$runCommandRaw` until the Postgres migration lands.

To actually learn what I was shipping, I asked for a `TERRAFORM_LEARN.md` walking through the files one by one, and dug into Aurora vs DocumentDB. I also mapped out current VPS pressure (8 GB, two Mongos, two Next.js containers, Dokploy) and compared it against a fully serverless path on Vercel + Supabase.

### Personal site
On the blog, I shipped page-level keyword meta tags and baked GEO rules directly into the blog-post skill, so future posts inherit the SEO defaults automatically.

### Side explorations
Sketched a PRD for an accounting/BPO product idea, comparing it against TOTVS, Sankhya, Protheus, Senior, and Conta Azul to find the actual gap.
