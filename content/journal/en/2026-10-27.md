Today was a heavy ship day on the ticketing platform, with nine commits landing across the stack after yesterday's cutover.

### Ticketing platform

The big feature work:

- **Absorbable platform fees** — organizers can now choose to eat the platform fee themselves instead of passing it to the buyer.
- **Terms of Use gate** — event creation now requires explicit acceptance of the ToU before the form opens.
- **Google Wallet integration** — tickets can be added straight to the Google Wallet, which is the kind of polish that moves the perceived quality of the product up a notch.
- **Backoffice access** — the internal team now has visibility across every organization, which should cut support turnaround dramatically.

The rest was post-launch hardening: keeping redirects pinned to the app's origin (no more `//host` escapes), making the security-headers test tolerate a stale ISR answer, clamping the minimum-per-purchase to actual capacity, preserving national phone numbers in the registration flow, and a grab-bag fix for headers, `workers.dev`, the `PAYMENT_DELETED` path, labels, and sharded E2E.

### Elsewhere

Spent some time poking at an OAuth integration flow in a side project (manual testing locally, chasing a client-id mismatch) and reading about forward-deployed engineering as a model. Both felt like useful context to carry into next week more than something to ship today.

Good rhythm. Launch stabilization is finally outnumbering launch firefighting.
