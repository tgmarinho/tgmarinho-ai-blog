A dense day across the pilot-ops platform, mostly landing the commissions model end to end and wiring a real accounting integration.

### Commissions pipeline

Merged the base commission per member and operational role in the registration layer, then propagated it forward: a snapshot of the schedule's commissions at plan time, and the apparent commission value surfaced in the activity detail. Also improved error feedback in the planning form so highlighted fields explain what's blocking a save. The design decision I locked in today: the registered percentage is the base, but the planner can override it per activity (technician, pilot, and auxiliaries individually). Turned that into a spec and tickets referencing the parent task, and validated the full flow on mobile.

### Conta Azul OAuth

Shipped OAuth connection per company in the backoffice, plus docs registering who can authorize the app. Callback route is live and the credentials handshake is working.

### Infra rethink

Started questioning the current cron setup. I want scheduled jobs triggered by us over an API rather than tied to the Railway scheduler. Spent time comparing Upstash and Inngest, particularly how Inngest models durable steps and retries.

### Docker portability

On a smaller repo, fixed a build failure by dropping a cache mount that wasn't portable across builders, and switched primary keys to native UUIDv7 with external id validation.
