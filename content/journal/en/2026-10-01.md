Today was less about shipping new code and more about steering the fleet. Across several parallel worktrees of the same project, the same batch of commits propagated: tenant group coverage, a cleanup for pending dialogs in the activities flow, enum alignment for unit of measure, discard and redirect fixes with exit blocking, and a backoffice tweak that puts CNPJ first with Receita autofill plus a fallback provider.

### PR triage

Most of the day was spent reviewing open PRs by priority: bug, fix, improvement, new feature. I identified which could be closed without merging, labeled the conflicted ones so another dev can resolve them, and flagged a regression that landed on main: a merged change missed the confirmation-cycle fix, so editing an assigned work order and swapping to an aircraft already booked that day blocks saving. The culprit is a hardcoded `confirmarTrocaEquipe: false` in `usePlanejamentoAtividade.ts`. I wrote the spec and opened an issue for a corrective PR to come first.

### Cloudflare detour

On a separate repo, I went deep into Cloudflare Workers: best practices, serverless fullstack reference architecture, pricing, and the `vinext` project. Thinking about whether queue-based async email delivery should stay on our current ingest or move to a native Cloudflare primitive. No decision yet — just mapping the terrain.
