Today was a heavy migration day across three repos.

### freetown

The big push: a full API migration from tRPC to native oRPC, done procedure by procedure — health, user, event, organization, order, ticket, checkin, coupon, secretLink, dashboard, inscritos, signature, financialOnboarding, ticketType, formField. Along the way I removed superjson, dropped the tRPC layer entirely, and moved request context and org authorization out of the old setup.

Auth got a parallel overhaul: NextAuth is gone, replaced by Better Auth, with the integration suite brought up to the new schema. I also laid down a Drizzle schema and per-request client next to Prisma to start that migration.

On the correctness side: atomic reservation of seat, coupon and secret link in `order.create`; constant-time webhook secret comparison; a race fix in check-in so each ticket admits once even under concurrent scans; and the fake payment provider now refuses to boot outside test deployments. Added a k6 load kit with five scenarios and published a baseline.

### skopje

Shipped fixes on the financeiro + Conta Azul integration: unified client field tied to the owner, de-duplicated descriptions, and separated excluded installments in the account detail. Documented the manual test script.

### munich-v2

Opened a new repo with the initial commit — iterating on a cover concept.
