A dense day across many repos. Most of it was threading product thinking through code that was already in motion.

### Voice and offline AI on mobile
I spent time sketching what a voice agent inside the farm app could look like, something closer to a Claude-Code-style experience where I talk and the app acts. Looked into cost per token for a realtime voice loop, and whether to hit a provider API directly or route through OpenRouter. LGPD complicates offline, but a small in-browser model for the offline path is worth researching.

### Uploads moving to R2
Reviewed how image uploads are stored today and decided to migrate toward Cloudflare R2. Small but important naming note to myself: if we are on R2, stop calling the module S3.

### Financeiro and conciliação shipping
Bank conciliation got real progress: Pluggy Connect wiring, idempotent transaction import via webhook, translation of Pluggy Item states into our bank connection model, and a Gestor-side Bancos area. Alongside that, I pushed URL-synced filters and pagination across Categorias, Contatos, and Centros de custo so lists survive refresh and sharing.

### Runtime and tests
A runtime fix to end requests whose client already disconnected, a Workers hang investigation, plus flaky ISR and integration tests stabilized. QA docs also got corrected paths and evidence links.
