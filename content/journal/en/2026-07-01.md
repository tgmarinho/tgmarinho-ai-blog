The day was about turning scattered work into structures that are easier to continue. The priority was shape: publish content, organize context, and compare technical paths before moving code.

### Site content

On the site, I published two bilingual posts. One explained HTTP QUERY, now RFC 10008, as a safe method with a body for large queries. The other compared Superpowers and Agent Skills with practical criteria: portability, maintenance, impact on real work, and evidence of use.

I also brought the missing June records into `/daily`. The goal was to keep the site as a living history of agent work, not only as a static portfolio.

In parallel, I revised an in-progress post about `AsyncLocalStorage` with a new reference on multi-tenancy in Node.js. The article became more grounded in concrete use cases: request context, logging, ORM integration, and framework limits.

### Personal OS

I moved the `personal-os` direction forward as a private context repository, not as a loose set of notes. I organized area indexes, added maps for agents, and simplified `CLAUDE.md` so it points to `AGENTS.md`.

I also moved notes about career, study, and content creation into versioned locations. The separation became clearer: long-term context goes into the repository, temporary workspace notes stay in `.context/`.

This was not about collecting more files. It was about reducing context loss when several agents work in parallel.

### Product migration

In another project, I compared an existing SaaS with a technical template before assuming a larger migration. The review looked at domain fit, stack fit, storage, ownership, and adapter limits.

The decision was to keep some current pieces for now, including the upload path, and treat the migration as incremental. It was better to record the compatibility conditions than to force a base change too early.

I also used agents to think more broadly about publishing strategy and technical positioning. The common thread was the same: organize what already exists before trying to expand.

It was a consolidation day. When the context is cleaner, the next decision feels lighter.
