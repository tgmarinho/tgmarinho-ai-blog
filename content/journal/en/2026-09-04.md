Today was less about shipping new features and more about tightening the seams on work already in flight across several parallel worktrees.

### Form and data hygiene
A recurring theme: storing values unmasked. Phones, CEPs, and CNPJs should live in the database without cosmetic formatting - masks belong to the UI layer, not the storage layer. I also wired up a ViaCEP lookup on an address form so users stop typing what an API can fill in for them, and stayed strict about pulling from the design system instead of reinventing spacing and components ad hoc.

### Debugging a JDBC connection
Spent time chasing a `Invalid JDBC URL` error in DBeaver against a local Postgres instance, followed by a `column "telefone" does not exist` on a query - a good reminder that schema drift between local branches bites fast when you're juggling multiple worktrees.

### Equipment selection edge case
Sketched out a fix for a workflow where a newly registered piece of equipment wasn't selectable in an offline-synced report. The right shape: let users pick from what exists; new records should flow through a manager, not a field form.

### Reflection
On the side, I sat with an idea worth carrying: being relevant to 100 people beats being known by a thousand. Purpose over reach.
