Today was a focused push on financial features across three worktrees of the same product. The three commits that landed cover two related fronts: a new **Financeiro** section in the tenant sidebar, and OAuth connection to Conta Azul scoped per company from the backoffice.

### Backoffice OAuth
The backoffice now handles the Conta Azul OAuth handshake on a per-company basis. Got the connection working end-to-end in production, which unblocks everything downstream. My rule of the day: get the connection green, park the follow-ups, keep moving. Solve blockers later instead of stalling on them.

### Sidebar and navigation
The **Financeiro** entry is now live in the tenant sidebar, giving the module a real home in the UI instead of living as a scattered set of routes.

### Mapping the receivables surface
With the integration in place, I started scoping what needs to come across from Conta Azul into our side before we can create accounts receivable and payable. The dependency list is already forming: cost centers, financial categories, bank accounts, an aircraft de-para, and contacts. Starting with **contas a receber** and walking the graph from there.

Small, boring, compounding progress. Exactly the kind of day I want more of.
