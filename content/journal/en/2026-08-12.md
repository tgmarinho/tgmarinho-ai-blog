Today was less about shipping code and more about reshaping the mental model of what comes next. Two repos, three sessions, zero commits - and that's fine. Some days the work is thinking out loud until the shape of the system clicks.

### ouagadougou

Straightforward housekeeping: merged the remote `main` into my working branch, resolved conflicts, and got the branch back in sync. Nothing glamorous, but keeping branches close to `main` avoids a lot of pain later.

### bissau

This is where most of the day went. I've been studying the existing SQL and SQLite dumps from a previous iteration to understand what already works, and using that as the base for a rebuild - this time as a proper multitenant SaaS.

A few shifts crystallized:

- **Membership as a first-class table.** Instead of coupling members directly to a single company, I want a `members` table with a relationship to companies. The same person can belong to more than one company - useful when someone is invited to collaborate across organizations. Roles I'm considering: manager, pilot, technician, support.
- **Reports become activity plans.** What used to be a single monolithic "report" in the legacy system maps naturally to an activity plan with 1:1 links to pilot and technician assessments. Every activity carries the pilot's evaluation alongside the technician's.

Next step is turning these notes into an actual schema draft and validating it against the legacy data before writing any migrations.
