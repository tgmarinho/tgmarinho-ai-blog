Today was a wide, parallel day. I had several worktrees open at once and pushed the same design direction through most of them while poking at bugs and polish along the way.

### Agropilot worktrees

Across amman, atlanta, bilbao, and phoenix, the headline change was applying the new NexaCRM-inspired UI and locking Dubai in as the single source-of-truth font. I also shipped a small but useful UX detail: activity durations now display in decimal hours alongside the hourmeter reading, which matches how the field actually reads time.

Beyond the merged commits, I spent time hunting for improvements in the PWA and offline flow, reviewing the project for security gaps and latent bugs, and sketching UI/UX upgrades for the pilot-facing agenda screen. I also chased down an on-grid spacing violation flagged by our design-system lint rule, which was a good reminder that the token system is starting to pay for itself.

### Pixou (oslo, bangui)

On the Pixou side I merged a chunk of visual work: a Luma-style background and header, a small `/efeitos` catalog page, a subtle glow aura on the window border, and Lenis-powered smooth scrolling. It is starting to feel like a real product surface rather than a demo.

### Tooling

Also set up Sentry at the project level following the official skill path, so upgrades stay boring.
