Big push day across a few repos. Most of the energy went into a company-groups feature and getting a proper staging environment online.

### Company groups and group reports

Landed the full "company groups" slice: group management in the backoffice, a group-aware company selector in the header (popover on desktop, card in the mobile menu), and a brand new Reports screen scoped by group with Financial and Operational tabs. On the backend, I added flown hours, productivity, and accrued commission to feed the Operational tab. Also tightened the admin selector to show only active companies with name-based search, and surfaced the Conta Azul connection status on the company list. Small but satisfying fixes along the way: currency masks on the Conference screen, and a PWA fix so the parecer opens offline on first session and rate limits stop blocking the SPA.

### Staging domain and email delivery

Flipped the auto-deploy to prod off and set up `staging.inscricoestop.com.br` as a real staging target, with room to think about PR previews next. On email, two correctness fixes: staging and preview envs can now email anyone (never reserved domains), and inline images are sent to the Email Service binding as bytes so the logo actually shows up.

Good day. Everything shipped, nothing lingering.
