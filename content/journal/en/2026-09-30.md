Busy day across five repos. Most of the energy went into backoffice ergonomics and financial plumbing, with a side quest into a new SaaS shape for a Bible RAG project.

### Backoffice and backend
Landed CNPJ-first company creation with autofill from the Receita, including a reserve provider so the form keeps working when the primary lookup falls over. On the backend, overload shedding now responds with a proper `503` and is disabled under tests to stop poisoning CI runs.

### Financial module
Shipped the "espelho" concept: mirrors, links, entries, and group-level reports wired to Conta Azul. Spent time digging into whether credit card reconciliation via Open Finance is feasible today, and what a direct Open Finance integration would actually require (licensing, aggregator choice, scope).

### Bible RAG
Reframed the backend into a pnpm monorepo with Drizzle and Biome, added SPEC and ADRs for a SaaS architecture, and made the dataset directory configurable via `BIBLES_DIR`. Thinking through Cloudflare + Neon as the hosting shape for a multi-tenant subscription product.

### Housekeeping
Merged staging into main on one project and reviewed a scheduled job built around `setInterval` with `onReady`/`onClose` hooks - good reminder that "cron-ish" in-process timers are often enough.
