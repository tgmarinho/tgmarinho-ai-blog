Big shipping day. Most of the movement happened across parallel worktrees on a private client codebase, all landing into the same trunk, plus a small maintenance touch on this site.

### Client project (private)

Twenty-six PRs merged across the day, spanning multiple concerns:

- **PWA hardening.** Documented a local offline test flow and revisited manifest/offline UX guidelines to make the install and disconnected experiences feel intentional.
- **Auth.** Centralized authentication into a single provider/context, renamed a stale `atualizado` variable to `usuarioAtualizado` for clarity, and drafted a plan to bootstrap the first admin safely in production.
- **Forms.** Rolled `react-hook-form` + `zod` across the frontend forms and audited the services layer. Left the two largest editors (auto-save, `useFieldArray`, offline sync) for dedicated PRs; the infrastructure is ready for incremental adoption.
- **Build & infra.** Migrated the Railway build from Nixpacks to Railpack, fixed a 500 on `DELETE /usuarios` triggered by an invalid profile row, and refactored domain enums to share a single source between frontend and backend.
- **Docs.** Added `CONTRIBUTING.md`, per-OS install instructions (macOS/Linux/Windows), a QA plan, and an architecture diagram split into `arquitetura/frontend` and `arquitetura/backend`.
