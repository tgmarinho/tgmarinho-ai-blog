Today was a modeling day. Less code, more thinking through the shape of the data before it hardens into schemas that are painful to change later.

### hong-kong-v4

The core question: how should a group of companies share (or not share) their catalogs? Prices, service types, cultures, equipment, helpers — some of these are commercial decisions that must stay per CNPJ, others are just reference data that's identical everywhere.

I landed on a "matriz" (parent) model: a single shared set of catalog entries at the group level, with each branch able to activate or deactivate what it uses. Pricing and commercial conditions stay per company, because that's where the curation actually matters. The single commit of the day captures this: a docs write-up on company groups and the financial layer.

Along the way I went down a few conceptual side-quests: what "Seams" actually means, what a Document is in the context of MongoDB or Firestore, and when Firestore is genuinely the right pick versus modeling chat (rooms, people, messages) as plain entities in Postgres.

I also realized I need a glossary. Terms like SPED come up and I have no intuition for what they are or how they're generated. Building that vocabulary is going to be part of the work.

Slow day, but the kind that saves weeks later.
