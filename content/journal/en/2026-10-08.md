Today was a wide-angle day: fixes landing in the field app, infra tightening, and a couple of structural moves that had been nagging me.

### Field app QA sweep

Ran a full QA pass and turned the findings into a single fix bundle. The highlights: the Pilotos and Técnicos tabs now include people who accumulate both roles, the Operador view no longer leaks the schedule commission, and the app shell revalidates the open company when connectivity comes back. I also updated the E2E mocks to match the current contract and bumped an integration timeout that was flaking.

### Infra

Preview-per-PR now provisions and seeds its own Postgres on each deploy. Isolating state per preview has been on my mind for a while, and getting there made the whole QA loop feel less fragile.

### Content studio

Realized the video tooling didn't belong inside a product repo. Spun it into a standalone, project-agnostic content studio and shipped a 15s showreel in both 16:9 and 9:16 as the first real output.

### Backoffice MCP

Started a backoffice team concept with an MCP server per organization — the idea being that each org gets its own agent-reachable surface instead of a single shared console.

Small day on paper, but a lot of the friction I'd been living with got shaved down.
