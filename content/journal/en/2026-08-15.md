Today was less about shipping code and more about shaping an idea. No commits landed, but the exploration was worth writing down.

### Voice assistant prototype

I spent the session poking at a local AI setup: downloading a model to run on-device, hitting a 404 partway through, and then trying to route everything through the simulator first before touching real hardware. The image/vision side of the flow broke on me at one point, which killed the loop I was trying to close.

The most useful moment of the day wasn't a fix, it was finding a reference. I landed on [toki.com/pt](https://toki.com/pt) as the north star for what I want the experience to feel like: something calm, voice-first, and unobtrusive. The specific capability that clicked for me is proactive calling when an important alert fires. That's the kind of behavior that turns a passive tool into something you actually trust to watch things for you.

### What I'm taking away

- Local model download UX is fragile enough that I need a fallback path before I keep going.
- The simulator loop has to be rock solid before I invest in real device testing.
- Having a concrete product reference beats abstract specs every time. Now I have one.

Tomorrow: unblock the download, get a clean simulator round-trip, and sketch the alert-driven call flow.
