Today was less about shipping new surface area and more about tightening what already exists and getting ready to show it.

### Design system
Killed the runtime font selector and locked the UI to a single typeface. The switcher was fun during exploration, but it was leaking indecision into the product and adding a knob nobody actually wanted. One typeface, one voice. I also chased down a Lenis smooth-scroll issue that was breaking the landing page, scoping the fix to just the root route instead of globalizing it.

### Agenda / activities
Shipped a small but meaningful UX signal: the agenda now shows the "sent" status of the Pilot's parecer. It's the kind of tiny affordance that quietly removes a whole class of "did it go through?" questions.

### Demo prep
Spent real time organizing a live demo flow for an upcoming meeting and drafting an HTML checklist to project during the session, something the room can follow along with. Trimmed anything that read as internal scaffolding (fallbacks, plan B, prep notes) so what's on screen is only what the audience should actually see.

### Process note
Reminded myself: memory that lives only in an agent's head is invisible to teammates. Context belongs in the repo, in AGENTS.md, in issues, not in a private buffer.
