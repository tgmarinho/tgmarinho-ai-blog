Today split cleanly into two tracks: research on a financial module and infra firefighting across a few services.

### Financial module research

Spent the morning walking through Conta Azul's UI, especially the Contas a Pagar and Nova Despesa flows, plus the quick-help panels. The goal was to figure out what a proper financial system needs to expose to integrate cleanly: accounts payable and receivable, service orders, recurring contracts, service and product invoices, and advances. I mapped required CRUDs against the endpoints and started consolidating everything into docs. First pass of the output was rough and I threw it out. Also started sketching multi-tenant shape where a company group has branches as tenants, and how an "assistant" role has to become a first-class entity instead of a side note.

### Infra and Docker

Landed the same fix across four repos: capping V8 heap in the Docker build stage to prevent the OOM killer from taking builds down. On the server I added a 4 GB swapfile so the 8 GB box has some breathing room during builds. Also started digging into how to auto-expire preview deployments on Dokploy, and weighed Bun vs npm for the build step. A staging branch pulled in new env vars, so I need to reconcile those on the infra side next.
