Today was mostly infrastructure hygiene across a few repos, plus a long overdue cleanup of my local machine.

### Branching and preview environments

I split the flow into a `staging` branch that promotes to `main` for production. PRs now target `staging`, and preview deployments reuse the staging database without running migrations - the `preDeployCommand` just prints a reminder instead of migrating. I also moved one of the projects to serve previews on `pr-N.preview.inscricoestop.com.br`, dropping the manual domain-renaming step.

### Migrations: one path, one person

Migrations were drifting into "whoever remembers runs them." I locked that down: `db:migrate` now refuses to run when a migration is out of order, `db:migrate:staging` explains failures instead of exiting silently, and I added a `db:verificar-migrations` shortcut at the root. The documented path is now the Railway Console, run by a single person, with a step-by-step `RAILWAY.md` describing a change end to end. Also added notes on how to check whether two environments share the same Postgres volume.

### Local cleanup

My disk was choking. I purged npm/yarn/pnpm/bun caches, dropped stale worktrees pointing at a repo that no longer exists, and cleared `node_modules`, `.next`, `.turbo`, `dist`, `build` and old `.git` histories from a handful of archived projects. Freed a lot of room, and sorted Downloads and Documents into something navigable again.
