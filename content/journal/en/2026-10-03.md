Today was one of those days where months of groundwork collapse into a single push.

### skopje

Landed the big Financeiro PR: full integration with Conta Azul, including the mirror, links, accounts, activity billing, and group reports. Then I fixed a bug where the consolidated report wouldn't stay coherent when all companies were unchecked, and updated README, AGENTS, and the product doc to reflect everything shipped. New backend envs went to the deploy target.

### freetown

The marathon. I finally removed Prisma from the whole app — client, seeds, scripts, migrations, Better Auth, webhooks, Inngest jobs, tickets, checkins, coupons, orders, events, organizations, members, the load kit, Playwright fixtures. Everything is on Drizzle now, with one shared client per Node process. The Prisma-era migrations still run, just without the Prisma CLI.

On top of that, I moved the runtime to Cloudflare Workers with vinext, declared the Hyperdrive binding, added a preview and deploy pipeline, Turnstile, rate limiting, a preview OAuth proxy, and structured logs. Storage moved from UploadThing to R2 through signed upload URLs, with a copy step for the old files. Daily DB backups to R2 with a monthly restore drill. A new sandbox-router Worker handles registration.

Thirty-eight commits. The stack feels lighter already.
