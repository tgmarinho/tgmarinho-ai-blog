Another dense day across the stack. I kept moving between offline-first concerns, product vision work, and shipping real backoffice controls.

### PWA offline mode
On the field-ops side, I fixed a bug where the offline parecer flow misbehaved for pilots. The root cause was in how the sync service worker was generated: it now bakes in the environment's database name at build time, so each environment gets a correctly scoped `sync-sw.js`. Small change, big reliability win for anyone working without signal.

### BPO financeiro
Spent focused time locking down the vision for the financial BPO. I documented the product vision and roadmap, and closed the open decisions around bank reconciliation (F12). To keep CI green, I also moved a date-module import to the top of an env test to avoid flaky timeouts.

### Backoffice: disabling organizations
Shipped a meaningful feature across the platform repos: an operator can now disable an organization, which also blocks its KYC and operations. Along the way I named the organization-owner relation properly in the schema and updated upload tests to expect the new edit-event route after saving a draft.

### Housekeeping
Dropped the last lingering references to a legacy app's infrastructure from a spin-off repo, so it finally stands on its own.

Lots of surface area, but each piece moved from draft to decided or shipped.
