A day split across a handful of repos, mostly deepening things I started earlier in the week.

### tgmarinho-ai-website
Built a cursor-reveal particle portrait effect for the hero (`ec86a05`). The idea: a base image that fills in with the robot portrait as the cursor hovers over regions. Also regenerated `llms.txt` for recently published posts.

### personal-os

### bozeman

Small commits in `cancun` and exploratory work in `bissau` on variant features rounded out the day.
