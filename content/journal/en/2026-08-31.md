Today was a consolidation day. Two separate observability efforts had been living side by side in different PRs, and it was time to stop having two half-answers to the same question: how do we see what's happening in production?

### Unified Sentry instrumentation

The main outcome landed as a single commit that merges PRs #512 and #461 into one coherent observability setup. Instead of two overlapping instrumentation layers competing for the same hooks and duplicating context, there is now one path for capturing errors, spans, and breadcrumbs. Less surface area, less drift, one place to reason about when something looks off in the dashboards.

### Syncing worktrees

With the unified instrumentation merged, I pulled the change down into the parallel worktrees I keep for feature work. Keeping those workspaces aligned with `main` (and with the shared demo baseline) is a small chore that pays off constantly: the moment a branch drifts, every "is this a real bug or just my stale local?" question costs an hour.

### Reflection

The interesting part is not the diff, it is the shape of the day: two workspaces, one shared concern, one merge. That is the pattern I want more of - collapse parallel efforts into one truth before they calcify into two competing conventions. Observability, of all things, should not have two answers.
