Today was less about shipping commits and more about absorbing context. Zero commits across three repos, but plenty of ground covered.

### Finance domain deep-dive

Spent a good chunk of the day learning accounting concepts from the ground up: what a DRE actually represents, how rateio works with a concrete example, and the difference between Categoria and Centro de Custo. Also skimmed the Conta Azul API docs and revisited OAuth 2.0 fundamentals. No code yet, just building the mental model I need before touching anything financial. It's the kind of prep work that saves days later.

### Running seeds locally

Got a project spun up with its separate Docker container for the database and executed a seed script to populate carga data. Nothing glamorous, but having a reliable local dataset is the difference between debugging blind and iterating fast.

### Porting features across stacks

Picked up a migration effort mid-flight, ported the remaining pieces, and then went back through both backend and frontend to double-check every feature actually made it across. This kind of audit pass is tedious but non-negotiable, one missed endpoint or component and the whole thing feels broken to users.

Days like this remind me that reading and verifying count as real work, even when the git log stays quiet.
