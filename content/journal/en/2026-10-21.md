Today was a split between shipping a concrete fix and thinking through two product flows.

### palikir
A receivable wasn't being created end-to-end, so I dug into the Conta Azul integration and switched it to the synchronous API, dropping the protocol-based send step. Since the project isn't live with real customers yet, I also took the chance to flag unused tables and fields for cleanup inside the same PR instead of carrying dead schema forward.

### salem
Thought through a bigger shift: today Dusty is the source that generates and sends accounts with an idempotency key. The idea on the table is letting invoice/receipt capture - with AI parsing - produce a payable or receivable request directly. The appeal is leaning on an endpoint I already pay for instead of standing up more infra on my side. For now it would live as a parallel table, and only "become" a formal payable once the request lands.

### windhoek
Worked on the ticketing model - how `Order` and `Purchase` relate, whether CPF should hard-block duplicate ticket purchases, and the rule that a coupon applies once per order. Also refined the UI: a per-ticket quantity stepper with a live total, except for single-attendee events where the stepper doesn't belong.

Small shipping day, but the modeling conversations will pay off next week.
