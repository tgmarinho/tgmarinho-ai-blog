Today was mostly about choosing structure over patchwork and shipping a lot of it.

### Frontend platform work

I committed to a foundation-first approach for the frontend refactor: instead of chasing pointwise fixes that would just become rework once the architecture moves, I landed the base first and let the framework choices absorb a lot of the small issues downstream.

Wave 2A went in: Auditoria, Lixeira, Admin and Usuarios were migrated into their per-feature folders, with Auditoria moving to React Query and gaining cleaner KPIs and hero. The Dashboard got its internal screens and components extracted, Playwright was wired up in the frontend, and the "novo relatório" flow was rebuilt as a feature with its own provider, zustand-backed state, standardized fields (radius, placeholders, lucide icons, DatePicker, currency mask), and a hydration/focus fix. Smaller wins: fixed the broken password eye on first access, moved health to a dedicated route, and added response contracts to auth.

### Infra and previews

Tightened how Vercel previews talk to the API - keeping wildcard preview URLs functional without opening prod CORS to arbitrary origins, and documenting the reasoning inline.

### Writing

Shipped a bilingual post on agentic git/GitHub workflows (including how agent-driven tools fit in) plus a branch-sync follow-up, and another bilingual pair on database connection pooling with PgBouncer/Neon.
