Today was a mix of deep focus on two products and a scattering of exploratory threads.

### Reconciliation (argonaut / velvetfish)

Seven commits across backend and frontend to land the bank statement feature: backend now stores the imported statement and persisted balance, a new screen renders it inside the accounts view, and I validated the whole thing against the real Pluggy sandbox. One round of fixes came directly from what the sandbox revealed, and another from reviewing the imported statement once it was on screen. I also stabilized integration test hooks that had been flaky.

### iTop Light

Nineteen commits pushing the Light tier toward a merge. Highlights: AI-assisted triage of payment receipts with a code-computed seal, participant-side receipt upload plus an organizer review queue, Pix flow with manual confirmation and expiry, simplified registration asking only for name and WhatsApp or email, backoffice reporting and suspension, and a full content rewrite for the Light's positioning. Docs and tests followed each feature in — proofs, retention, the review queue, and the Pix settings.

### Side quests

Started scoping a Rust learning path for a vibecoder with web experience, sketched a mobile vs web decision for a Ridley idea, and kicked off a video-maker tool for roteiro feedback with audio input. Light seeds, but worth revisiting.
