Today was more about a personal knowledge base than a public product. I used agents to capture study, turn media into text, and think about how to make that flow less manual.

### Personal OS

I organized English class notes and saved study material in Markdown. I also worked on private career documents, but those stay out of the public journal by design. The public record is only the working pattern: turn loose information into versioned, reusable files.

The same pattern showed up in technical reading and small questions during the day. Instead of letting every answer disappear in chat, I moved the useful parts into the right repository.

### Transcripts and local study

Another theme was transcription. I tested ways to extract content from videos, keep cited links, and prepare a flow similar to a local study notebook. The idea was still exploratory, but it started to become clear: audio and video become raw material, then an agent helps organize meaning, references, and next steps.

A simple operational decision also appeared: shortcuts and folder structure matter. The faster I can capture a source, the more likely it is to become useful knowledge.

It was an internal workshop day: less visible delivery, more sharpening of the system that turns study into action.
