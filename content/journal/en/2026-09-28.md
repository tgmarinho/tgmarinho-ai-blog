Today was less about shipping code and more about thinking through an integration design that has been sitting on the backlog for a while.

### Integration reference

I went back to an integration reference proposal we had sketched out some time ago. Reopening an old thread is always a bit of an archaeology exercise: rebuilding the mental model of why we shaped it the way we did, and checking whether the reasoning still holds up now that the surrounding system has evolved.

The main question I kept circling was what a financial transaction record actually represents in this context, and how it relates to the accounting-side entry it eventually becomes. The two concepts look similar on the surface but they live at different layers, and conflating them tends to leak accounting concerns into places that should stay purely operational.

Working through it with a couple of reference screenshots helped me align the shape of the data with the flow I want. By the end of the session I felt the direction was solid enough to move on.

No commits today, and that is fine. Some days the real output is a clearer picture of the problem, and that is what makes the eventual implementation cheap and boring instead of a slog. Tomorrow I can start turning the sketch into something concrete.
