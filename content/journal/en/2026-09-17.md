Today was mostly modeling work on a client backend. I spent the session pinning down how master records should live at the group level while each company still owns its own bindings, and turning that into diagrams I can actually defend.

### Architecture

The core decision: a `grupo` table on top, `empresa` beneath it, and shared master entities (aircraft, runways, clients) hanging off the group. Each company then links to those masters through join tables like `empresa_aeronave` and `empresa_cliente`. The reason is that the external accounting system treats records as strictly per-company, so we need an explicit binding layer to reconcile the two worlds without duplicating data.

I refined the financial side too, moving toward a unified contact entity and a single `movimento_financeiro` stream, with read-only mirrors for the pieces that must stay in sync with the external source of truth. Three commits captured the progression: an initial pass on shared group registries plus the base of the financial integration, then the full architecture write-up with operational analysis and diagrams, and finally a tightening of the target financial model.
