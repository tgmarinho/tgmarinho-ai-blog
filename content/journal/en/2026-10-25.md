Today was a focused day on the registration platform, pushing a batch of hardening work across infra, CI, and payment flows.

### little-rock-v1

I landed security headers on every response, which closes a gap I had been meaning to address for a while. Small change on paper, meaningful posture improvement in practice.

On the deploy side, I set up a redirect so `workers.dev` pages now bounce to the environment's actual domain. No more stray subdomains showing up where they shouldn't. Alongside that, I worked out the WWW redirect through Cloudflare's Redirect Rules template, since the `www` host was down and needed to point back to the root.

The CI pipeline got a nice upgrade: the E2E suite now runs in four shards and merges the reports at the end. Feedback is faster and the suite stops being the slowest step in the loop.

On the payments side, I fixed two subtle bugs. The "amount paid" is now only reported once the order is actually paid - no more premature values. And when Asaas deletes a charge, the webhook properly cancels the pending purchase instead of leaving it dangling.

Finally, a small but real UX fix: registration now accepts phone numbers from any country. The previous validation was too strict for international users.

Thirteen commits, one busy afternoon.
