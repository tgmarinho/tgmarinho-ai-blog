Today was a mix of cleanup, direction-setting, and architectural thinking. Nothing flashy, but the kind of day that makes the next week easier.

### Test suite alignment

The end-to-end suite had drifted from the actual UI after we disabled the autopilot flow and removed autosave from drafts. I adjusted the specs to match reality: tests now save drafts by explicitly clicking the button instead of waiting on a ghost autosave that no longer exists. Small change, big signal - green CI stops being a lie.

### Product vision, written down

I finally committed a `VISION.md` capturing where the product is heading. Having it in the repo (rather than in my head) means every future decision - features, refactors, trade-offs - has something to be measured against. It also unblocks anyone reading the codebase cold.

### Multi-tenant vs single-tenant

Spent time thinking through the tenancy model. Today the data effectively serves a single company, but the schema carries the shape of multi-tenant. The path forward is doable in code as long as queries are disciplined about scoping. I want to study how a reference template handles this pattern before committing to a direction.

Two repos, eight commits, and a clearer sense of what to build next.
