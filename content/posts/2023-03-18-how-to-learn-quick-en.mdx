Learning quickly comes down to efficient use of time and effective study techniques. A few tips that work for me:

1. **Set clear goals**: before learning something, define specific, achievable, measurable objectives.

2. **Learn actively**: instead of just reading and highlighting, engage with the material — take notes, summarize, and teach the concept to someone else (even a rubber duck).

3. **Focus on what matters**: identify the essential information and spend your time and energy there. Don't get lost in irrelevant details.

4. **Use visual aids**: diagrams, mind maps, and other visuals help your brain anchor information.

5. **Take breaks**: short breaks recharge focus between sessions.

6. **Sleep enough**: poor sleep harms your ability to learn and retain information.

7. **Practice regularly**: consistent practice consolidates knowledge and builds skill. Spend time every day on what you're learning.

8. **Exercise**: regular physical activity improves cognitive function and accelerates learning.

9. **Eliminate distractions**: turn off notifications, avoid multitasking. Multitasking destroys focus.

10. **Use AI as a study tool**: ChatGPT, Claude and other LLMs are great for explaining concepts in different ways, generating exercises, and checking your reasoning. Use them as study partners, not crutches.

Remember: learning quickly doesn't mean learning everything in a short time. It means using effective techniques and setting realistic goals to optimize the process.

Never stop learning.
