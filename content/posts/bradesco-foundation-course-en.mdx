This site is a good option for people who have discipline, are self-taught, and enjoy reading. Each course has a deadline that depends on the workload; if you enroll and don't finish, you fail. For example, today I finished the UML (_Unified Modeling Language_) course, with a 45-hour workload across five modules, and I had 30 days to complete it. I worked on it every day, spent more than 2h/day, managed to read the material, got a good grasp of the subject, took the exam, and passed.
