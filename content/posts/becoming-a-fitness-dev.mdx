# Becoming a fitness Dev

The Five Do's for good daily habits that you should begin today.

1.  **Cardio** exercises every day, at least ten thousand steps a day
    
2. **Hydrate** - three-liter water per day 
    
3. **Diet** rich in protein - 2 grams x Your weight (sample: 2gr x 80kg = 160gr of protein daily)
    
4. **Workout** at the gym or with your own body, for instance, calisthenics
    
5.  **[Control](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/pt)** your daily [intake](https://fitnessflexivel.com.br/calculadora-tdee-macros/)
